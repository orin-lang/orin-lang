- **Trap-safe structured cleanup (roadmap C2).** A task or scope body that traps no longer leaves its children behind: the runtime cancels their scopes, wakes descendants blocked in a channel or scope wait so they end, and joins them all before the trap reaches the joiner or the process exits (status 101); a task that traps also cancels its scope so its siblings stop at their next `isCancelled()` or network wait; when several tasks of a scope trap, the first one is reported ("task panicked: ...") with the later ones after it ("also trapped: ...").
