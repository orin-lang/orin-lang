- **Compiler callees state what they return, round 2 (ADR-0053 Addendum A, group U1).** The compiler sources get the remaining O(1) clauses that callers' proofs need, in the directories that were left open: `isBool`, `isOrdinaryMode`, `isAlpha` (the body as a result equation), `isNormalized` (`"."` and `"/"` are normalized, the empty path is not), `mkExpr` (the span is the one given), `isWellFormed` (a well-formed program has an entry unit that is the last unit and one source file per unit), and the empty or one-element cases of `distinctPaths`, `spansDistinct`, `defIdsDense`, `isOccurrence`, `isDefName`, `rootsWellFormed`, `holesInRange` and `loopsOrdered`; the loops of the first three get `@Invariant` bounds (no runtime cost). `verify/state.or` `mk` requires `src <= 2^59` so that `src * 16` cannot overflow (every caller passes 0 or a local index). No contract was removed or weakened and none costs more than a few comparisons under `--check-contracts`.
