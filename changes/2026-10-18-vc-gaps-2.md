- **Verifier: more inferred loop counters and mixed-width integer equality (ADR-0036).** `orin verify --contracts` infers the counter bound from any conjunct of a `while` condition (`i < xs.length && xs[i] < 0`), proves these hidden loop obligations first from their own premises (the counter hypotheses, the loop condition and the facts they depend on) so that they no longer time out in long functions, and models `==` / `!=` on integers of different kinds (`usize` against `int`) as equality of the widened values instead of giving up.
