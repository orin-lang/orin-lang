- **Unique receivers and results across calls (ADR-0038 addendum, lever 2 T5, MIR v2.7).** Two new `MFunction` flags, `unique-in` (the in/out receiver is `Unique` on entry) and `unique-out` (the value handed back is `Unique`), are set by the optimiser (`compiler/opt/uniqflags.or`, optimistic assumption with retraction) and re-checked by the verifier on both sides of every call (rules U2-U5), so a `makeunique` that every caller has already paid for is not repeated in the callee and the result of a `unique-out` call needs none; the emitters ignore the flags. It also closes a hole where a borrowed `Unique` argument stayed `Unique` after an in/out call. The mutation corpus gains the `uniq-*` classes (`tools/mutate_mir.py`). mutres runs about 5.6% fewer instructions, the other benchmarks are unchanged.
