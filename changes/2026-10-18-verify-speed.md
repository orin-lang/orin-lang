- **Faster `orin verify --contracts`.** The solver queries of a round run on a dynamic worker pool (`xargs -P`) instead of a fixed share per thread, a query asked twice runs once, unrolled-loop obligations whose counterexample could not be trusted anyway are no longer sent to z3, and the new `--cache-dir DIR` (with `--cache-unknown`, `--solver-stats`) keeps z3's answers keyed by the SHA-256 of solver version, limits and query text, so a rerun only solves what changed; no status changes.
