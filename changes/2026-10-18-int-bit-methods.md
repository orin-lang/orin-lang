- **Bit operations on integers (reference TYP-6a).** Every integer type has `bitAnd`, `bitOr`, `bitXor`, `bitNot`, `shl`, `shr`, `rotl` and `rotr` as methods (no new syntax): they act on the two's complement pattern of the type's width, inline in both backends. `shl` and `shr` trap with `shift count out of range` unless `0 <= n < width`; rotations take the count modulo the width. This is what hashes and ciphers (`std:crypto`) need; the operators `& | ^ << >>` stay a separate syntax decision.
