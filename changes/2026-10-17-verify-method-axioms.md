- **Contract verifier models more array and string methods (ADR-0053 Addendum A, group U8).** `orin verify --contracts` no longer gives up with "array method `sort` is not supported" and its relatives: `find` / `findIndex` are modelled exactly, `sort`, `reduce`, `split` (with an obligation that the separator is not empty) and `indexOf` by sound partial axioms, and `startsWith` / `endsWith` / `includes` are now functions of their two strings (the same test in the body and in a contract agrees; length, empty, equality and prefix-implies-substring axioms; also allowed inside quantifier bodies) The bytes of short string literals (up to 16) are known to `charCodeAt`, so a precondition that reads the characters of a code literal can be proved (`compiler/vc/methods.or`, documented in `docs/contracts.md`). Claims the axioms do not prove stay UNKNOWN, never REFUTED.
