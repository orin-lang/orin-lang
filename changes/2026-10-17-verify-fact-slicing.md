- **`orin verify --contracts` asks a sliced query first (`--no-slice` turns it off).** A query used to assert every fact of the function next to the goal (0.1-0.8 MB for the larger functions of the compiler). When the facts that share no constant with the goal, the path condition and the assumptions, directly or through other facts, are a quarter of them or more (`compiler/vc/slice.or`), the goal is first asked with only the relevant facts and a quarter of the wall time: `unsat` is a proof (the slice only drops assumptions), anything else (`sat`, `unknown`) is not used and the full query is asked as before, so a counterexample is only ever reported from the full query and no VERIFIED can turn into UNKNOWN or REFUTED. Measured on the compiler (`--timeout-ms 2000`; numbers in docs/contracts.md): the facts of these queries are linked through the symbolic state, so slices are small only for a few queries and none of the 211 timeouts became a proof. Documented in docs/contracts.md.
